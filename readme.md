# Pixcation

Hello! Welcome to the world of Pixcation. What started out as a simple, pixelated vacation (where the name comes from, by the way) has now turned into something more. 

Start by talking to the guide to figure out the general gameplay loop and controls for the game. Or you greet her and leave. Your choice. Whatever you do, go over to the shop and buy your first rod. With your hotbar slot (1-9) selected on the fishing rod, hold left click or space to cast your rod.

Once your road is cast, wait for a fish to get hooked. You'll notice your bobber splash up and down, and then go down with small indicator lines once it's fully hooked. Pressing space again reels it in, where you need to play a tiny minigame to earn your fish. The harder the fish is to catch, the faster it will move during this minigame. So stay on your toes!

# Art

As two creators plagued by the nostalgia of our 2000s upbringing, we decided to take a pixel-art-meets-Animal-Crossing approach to the game. You'll notice certain aspects of the game really heavily reflect this (such as the visual appearance of the fish!)
